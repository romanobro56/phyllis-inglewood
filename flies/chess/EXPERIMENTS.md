# Local learning study protocol

This is the original mate-one study. Later controlled learning experiments and current conclusions are in [MECHANISM_PROTOCOL.md](MECHANISM_PROTOCOL.md), [MECHANISM_RESULTS.md](MECHANISM_RESULTS.md) and [COMPLETION.md](COMPLETION.md).

This protocol was fixed after a two-update validation-only implementation pilot and before running the 60-update studies or opening the test split. It is a local engineering study, not an external preregistration.

The question is whether the unchanged actual FlyWire `tiny` FlyBrain can improve first-move checkmate recognition through on-policy outcomes on a restricted tactical distribution. It is not whether it plays good full chess.

- Corpus generator: `curriculum.make_corpus(seed=731)`. 256 training, 64 validation, 128 test positions. KQK/KQRK starts with a legal mate in one; no more than 15% immediate stalemate moves. Starts are curated using legal outcomes, not an external engine. Eight board symmetries and color/current-player mirrors define one family; families never cross splits. Policies never see mate labels or a ranked move list. Answers are used for corpus curation and reporting only.
- Three main training seeds: 11, 23, 37. Two independent learners each; 60 updates, 128 genuine two-brain games/update, four-ply compute cap, Adam lr=.003, entropy=.003, gamma=1. Roles, colors and historical/live opponents balanced. Terminal +1/-1/0; unfinished games get zero curriculum outcome reward and are separately marked truncated, not drawn. Attacker/defender EMA baselines use prior updates only. No material shaping or teacher losses.
- Checkpoint measurements: updates 0, 10, 20, 30, 40, 50, 60; 64 validation positions with two color-balanced games each per learner. Final checkpoint 60 is fixed; validation does not select the best checkpoint.
- Main encoding: packed board and unchanged metadata plus 11 redundant geometric features in previously reserved floats (own/enemy king coordinates, own queen/rook coordinates, selected source piece type and displacement to enemy king). No new information, strategic score, connection, neuron or output head is introduced. Full board packing remains lossless and obs_dim remains 64.
- Encoding ablation: seed 11 with the original packed-only input, identical hyperparameters and initial weights. This is one-seed exploratory evidence; no strong causal encoding claim is permitted from it.
- Final test: all 128 test families, four sampled games/family (alternating actual colors), 512 games per condition per learner. Compare trained checkpoint, that learner's exact untouched initialization, uniformly random legal candidate, actual lesioned trained brain, and trained brain with shuffled board features. Opponent is a separate frozen initialization of the other learner. Full legal continuations are played through the cap. Random moves are evaluation baselines only; training uses FlyBrain for both sides.
- Primary endpoint: fraction of first moves that produce library-verified checkmate. Secondary: W/L/D/truncation counts, illegal attempts, subsequent win rate and stage probability/legality metrics. A capped game remains result `*`; mate failure is not a fabricated chess loss/draw.
- Uncertainty: bootstrap entire position families, preserving paired agent comparisons and repeated color/seed observations within each family. Aggregate learners within training seed. Report each seed and a pooled result, with a two-way bootstrap of training seeds and position families. Use simultaneous 98.75% intervals for the four trained-minus-control contrasts (Bonferroni familywise 5%). Three training seeds limit inference about initialization variation.
- Improvement language: claim tactical-distribution improvement only if trained beats both matching initialization and random legal controls with simultaneous intervals above zero. Attribute it to observation-dependent computation only if intact also exceeds actual lesion and shuffled-board controls. If those conditions fail, report the failure. No Elo or general chess intelligence claim.
- Diagnostics: record each parameter family's gradient norm and update norm, empty trajectories, forced stages, reward-bearing episodes, optimizer/RNG states, checkpoint and graph identity. Regression tests cover discovered normalization, reporting and gradient hygiene issues, batching equivalence, reward signs, checkpoint compatibility and split integrity.

Executed results and limitations are appended to AUDIT.md. Artifacts live under ignored `flies/chess/artifacts/learning/`. No GPU, shared infrastructure edit, commit or push is authorized by this study.

## Matched-board and progression study

The subsequent four-phase research study is defined in [RESEARCH_PROTOCOL.md](RESEARCH_PROTOCOL.md), with measured results and reproduction commands in [RESEARCH_RESULTS.md](RESEARCH_RESULTS.md). It uses new exact-full-legal-tree matched pairs, three intact and three independently restricted training runs, harder curricula, and frozen checkpoint comparisons. `research_results.json` and `research_evidence.json` preserve aggregate and per-family evidence; `research_data/` preserves the frozen datasets. Board-adaptation and harder-task gains remain inconclusive. The full-graph experiment in [SCALING.md](SCALING.md) is proposed only; no GPU job was run or is recommended yet.

## Exploratory follow-up (after the first test evaluations)

The corrected shuffled-board control retained high tactical success, despite zero same-board donors on first-move stages. This suggests much of the gain may come from stage-dependent action priors plus legal masking/partial choices. To investigate, add one explicitly exploratory seed-11 run with **stage-only observations**: retain the five stage flags and zero every other input. The same actual FlyBrain, eight descending groups, on-policy reward, data split, 60×128 games, and hyperparameters are used. This diagnostic is not the normal chess encoder and does not replace either player's brain. Its validation/test results are descriptive follow-up, not part of the original four-contrast confirmatory analysis. No main-run weights or hyperparameters were changed after observing test results.

The initial shuffle implementation shifted adjacent repeated rows by one, accidentally preserving most board identities. Those first reports are retained as `evaluation.invalid-shuffle-control.json`; they are excluded from conclusions. A tested board-identity derangement replaced the row shift, and all fixed checkpoints were reevaluated. This changes the null control, not trained weights, curricula, evaluation games/seeds, or the primary endpoint.
